Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

CodeConClave AI ("CodeConClave", "we") is an AI workspace made in India. This page says, in plain words, what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The short version: we use your data only to run CodeConClave for you. We never sell it, we show no ads, and we never use one person's data for someone else.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, email, and your password - stored only as a scrambled hash we cannot read.
  • What you save: your chats and memory (so they appear on your other devices). Incognito chats are never saved.
  • Team and Organization spaces: notes, shared chats, bills and reminders you add. Only the members of that space can see them.
  • Usage counts: how many messages you sent in your current window, so the free limit works.
  • Guests (not signed in): a random cookie ID and a scrambled form of your internet address, only to count free messages. These counts are deleted after 8 days.
  • Location: only when you ask something like "near me", and only for that answer. We do not store it.
  • Files and pictures you attach: read for that message only.
  • Voice: your recording is turned into text for that message and not kept.
  • Gmail (only if you connect it): your app password is stored encrypted. Mail is read or sent only when you ask, and sending always needs your yes.

What we never take

CodeConClave never asks for card numbers, OTPs, PINs, CVVs, passwords, Aadhaar or PAN in a chat. If you type one by mistake, it is hidden before it reaches the AI, and you are warned.

How we keep it safe

  • All traffic uses HTTPS.
  • Passwords are hashed; connected secrets are encrypted; API keys are kept only as a fingerprint.
  • New accounts confirm their email with a code.
  • Team data stays inside the team.

How long we keep it

Your account data stays until you delete it or ask us to. Guest message counts are deleted after 8 days. Team and Organization owners can set how long their space keeps things.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent. You can export or delete your chats yourself in Settings. For anything else, email medidisaharsh@gmail.com from your account's email. We reply within 7 days and finish a deletion within 30 days.

Grievances: write to medidisaharsh@gmail.com with the subject "Grievance". If you are not happy with our answer, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

If you are under 18, please use CodeConClave with your parent's or guardian's permission.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the date at the top. Big changes are also shown in the app.
